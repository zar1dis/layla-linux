# Character asset attribution

The Layla application source does not include the character model, textures,
or rendered animation frames.

The existing Layla artwork was created from Massimo Javer's **Base Mesh Female
with Rig and Textures**, published on Sketchfab:

- Model: <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/base-mesh-female-with-rig-and-textures-cae1b75bea294b6bae9ab5d06c086089>
- Artist profile: <https://sketchfab.com/@b5b60dce05b24a7c8de1d8f172f946>

Massimo Javer will always be credited as the creator of the original model.
The license terms for redistribution of the model and derivative artwork must
be verified against the model's current Sketchfab listing or with the artist
before any of those assets are bundled or published.
